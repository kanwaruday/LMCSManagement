Transport views: three directions

Mock-ups for choosing a look before anything is built. Real GPS data (25 Aug–23 Oct 2026, the 9 buses with working trackers) and the fleet roster. No driver or student names. Each view is built to answer specific decisions.

A. Metro map (Mini Metro)

Merge two routes? Where lines share stationsDrop or move a stop? Stations a few minutes apart on one lineWhich lines lose money? Line badge

Tap a station or a line

Each bus is a line in its own colour, drawn the way a metro map is: straight runs and 45° turns, stations in their real order but evenly spaced. Thick lines carry more children. Dashed lines have a tracker problem, so their stops are incomplete. Rings are stations two buses share: the first places to look at merging. Squares are where a bus waits (school, parking).

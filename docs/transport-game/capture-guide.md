# Capture portal: guide for Coordinators and Fee Clerks

The page is `coordinator/transport/capture.html`: Coordinators open it from Transport checks → **Capture portal**, Fee
Clerks from the Office portal's Live Buses → **Capture portal**. You ride the bus with a
school phone. The page saves everything on the phone and you send it later on school Wi-Fi.

## What you need

- **The school phone**, in Chrome. Sign in with your own LMS Google account (the one on the portal's access list).
- **A dashboard mount** that holds the phone on its side (landscape), camera looking out through the windscreen.
- **A charger**: a car charger and a cable long enough to reach the mount. The screen stays on for the whole run.

## Before the ride (on school Wi-Fi)

1. Open the page once on Wi-Fi. It loads the buses and their known stops and keeps them for the ride.
2. Sign in if asked. A sign-in lasts about an hour. If it has run out on the bus with no signal, the page still opens
   for capture ("Offline mode: sign in to send") as long as you signed in on this phone in the last 14 days; sign in
   again on Wi-Fi to send.
3. When Chrome asks, **allow the camera and the location**.

## Two kinds of run

| Run | When | Camera | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Timelapse** (default) | **Any time**, with the camera facing the road and no children's faces in the frame | Yes: a frame every 10 m, and a photo at each stop | Tap **STOP HERE** at each stop: one tap, nothing to answer |
| **Count run** | A normal school run | **Never** | Tap **STOP HERE** at each stop, count how many children got on and off |

Privacy:
- The page asks before every timelapse: "Camera faces the road · no children's faces in the frame". Tick it only if it is true. Faces and number plates are still blurred on the Mac before anything is shared.
- A count run records numbers only, never children's names, and never uses the camera.
- Every photo is private and is blurred (faces, number plates) before anyone sees it.

## During the run

- Pick **Timelapse** or **Count run**, pick the bus, and tap **Start**.
- The top line is the mission, for example "Capture Route 18: 6 of 14 stops tagged", with one pip per stop under it.
- With the phone on its side in the mount, the run fills the screen: the camera on the left, **STOP HERE** on the
  right. STOP HERE stays at the bottom all run: a message takes room from the camera, never pushes the button away.
- The line under the camera shows the GPS. Amber means waiting or weak ("frames paused"): frames resume by themselves.
- **STOP HERE**: tap it once when the bus stops. It saves the GPS point, the time and a photo from the running camera, with nothing to answer ("Stop 3 saved with a photo (±8 m)").
  Stops are named later from the map. On a count run it still asks which stop and how many got on and off.
- **Keep the page open and the screen on.** If the phone locks or you switch apps, capture pauses. When you come back it
  carries on, and the time away is marked as a gap.
- If the page was closed, open it again: it offers **Carry on** for the open run, with that run's kind already picked.
- Count run: miscounted the last stop? Tap **Change count** under the number on board, put the numbers right, and save.
- Leaving the page during a run (the back link, a reload) asks first: the camera and GPS stop while you are away.
- **End run** needs two taps, so a bump cannot end it.
- "The phone is full": go to the **Upload** tab and send on Wi-Fi, then carry on. Nothing already saved is lost.

## The monthly safety check (Inspection)

- With the bus parked, pick **Inspection** and the bus. The line under the help says how big the check is, for example
  "10 items, 4 need a photo". Photograph the item only, never children or people.
- One card per item of the Owner's safety loadout: **Yes** (it is there and works) or **No** (missing or broken). A Yes may
  need a photo and the date on its certificate or tag; the card says what it still needs, in amber.
- The strip of slots at the top stays in view and fills in green (on board) or red (missing). Tap a slot to go to its card.
- A finished card folds to one line once you move on to the next one; tap the line to open it again.
- **Save safety check** when every slot is filled. Each missing item becomes a follow-up for the bus once it is sent.

## After the run

- The count run's summary shows the most children on board at once, against the seats and against the load cap
  (1.4 × seats).
- Back on school Wi-Fi, open **Upload** and tap **Send now on Wi-Fi**. Each run shows, for example, "312 of 540 frames
  sent". If the connection drops, tap Send again later: it carries on where it stopped and never sends a frame twice.
- A run is done when it says **Sent.** The run then appears in the Capture Runs tab of the transport workbook.
- The phone is shared. Anyone signed in on it can send any run waiting there; the run stays credited to the person who
  captured it (the Captured By column). An unfinished run or safety check can be carried on, ended or discarded only by
  the person who started it; others see only how many are waiting. If someone else signs in, the page starts again as theirs.

## Phone check before the first real use (Uday or a Coordinator, outside school hours)

Do this once on the school phone; it cannot be checked from a computer.

- [ ] A timelapse of about 1 km with the phone in the mount: frames count up as the bus moves, and stop when it stands.
- [ ] Two stops tagged with STOP HERE: each is one tap and says "Stop N saved"; a quick second tap saves nothing.
- [ ] Lock the phone for a minute mid-run and unlock it: the page says the time away is marked as a gap, frames resume.
- [ ] The screen stays on for the whole run (wake lock) while charging.
- [ ] Upload on school Wi-Fi: the count reaches "N of N frames sent" and the run says Sent.
- [ ] The run's row appears in the Capture Runs tab (with the gap and both stops), and its photos are in the bus's
      Capture folder in Drive.
- [ ] A short count run: no camera prompt at all, the counter works with one hand, the summary shows the peak load.
- [ ] A safety check with a photo item, saved and sent: it says Sent, and its row in Capture Runs is `ended` with the
      answers and Captured By = the person who did the check, also when someone else signed in to send it.

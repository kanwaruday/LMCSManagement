# Data request for vehicle agencies' service centres

**From:** La Montessori School (LMCS), transport department.
**Purpose:** set up a maintenance record per bus: service schedules, part lives and prices. That lets us
service our school buses on time and budget for parts.
**How to send it:** one reply per vehicle model, as Excel or CSV if possible, with our vehicles listed by
**chassis number**. PDFs of the official manuals and charts are fine too.

Send one copy of this list to each agency (Tata, Ashok Leyland, Force, Eicher, Mahindra, Maruti, as
applicable), with our list of their vehicles: registration number, chassis number and model. The staff
questionnaire collects those.

## 1. Our vehicles with you

For each of our chassis numbers:
1. Model, variant, engine type and emission norm (BS-IV / BS-VI), year of manufacture.
2. Warranty: what is still covered, and until when (date and km).
3. Any open recalls or service campaigns.
4. **Full service history from your system**: every job card with its date, odometer reading, work done,
   parts replaced (part name and number) and amount charged.

## 4. Prices and packages

1. The price of each **scheduled service package** (e.g. the 10,000 km service), with what it includes.
2. Labour rates (per hour or per job).
3. The **genuine** part price, and any approved alternative.
4. Annual maintenance contract (AMC) options for a fleet, and the price.
5. Pick-up and drop, or on-site service, for our campuses.

## 5. Running norms (per model)

1. Expected fuel economy (km per litre) at full school load on hill roads.
2. Recommended tyre pressure, and the rotation interval.
3. The official **seating capacity** per the registration (RC), and any rules for carrying children.

## 6. Contacts

The service advisor's name and phone, workshop timings, the nearest workshop to each campus (Dhalpur,
Kelheli, Dunkhra, Ner Chowk, Sayoli, Jogindernagar), and how to book.
